Gibson Assembly
===============

Keep all reagents on ice throughout the procedure.

1. Assemble the reaction in the order listed below, adding the HiFi Master Mix last to minimize wastage of enzymes if the reaction assembly needs to be repeated.

====================  ==============================================
Component             Volume
====================  ==============================================
Fragment(s)           1 µL (0.5 µL + 0.5 µL) of equal molar parts 
Nuclease Free Water   1.5 µL **                                   
Hi-Fi MM              2.5 µL                                       
====================  ==============================================

.. note::

   ** Adjust the volume of nuclease-free water as needed to maintain a final
   reaction volume of 5.0 µL. For assemblies containing more than two DNA
   fragments, reduce the volume of nuclease-free water accordingly to
   compensate for the additional fragment volumes.

2. Incubate the reaction at 50 °C for 1 hour.
