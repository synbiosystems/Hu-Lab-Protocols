Using the Department's Communal Autoclave
========================================

General
-------

* Sign the autoclave logbook or signup sheet before starting a cycle. This helps identify users if any issues arise with the autoclave.
* Use autoclave-safe containers and materials only.
* Do not overload the chamber. Leave sufficient space for steam to circulate around all items.
* Apply autoclave indicator tape to an appropriate place on the item. This helps confirm that the load has been exposed to the sterilization cycle.
* Select the appropriate preset autoclave cycle for your load. For liquid media, refer to the chart posted in the autoclave room to determine the correct cycle.
* An autoclave coordinator is responsible for maintenance and general oversight of the communal autoclave. This role rotates each semester. Report any equipment malfunction or abnormal cycle to the current coordinator.

Pipette Tip Boxes
-----------------

* Pipette tip boxes should remain completely closed during autoclaving.
* Place a strip of autoclave tape across the opening of the box (bridging the lid and the base) to help keep the box closed and serve as an exposure indicator.

Microcentrifuge Tubes
---------------------

* Place microcentrifuge tubes in an autoclave-safe tube box.
* Do not completely close the tube box to allow steam penetration.
* Secure the partially open lid with a strip of autoclave tape placed across both the container and the lid.

Glass Flasks
------------

* Cover flask openings with aluminum foil.
* Secure the foil by placing autoclave tape across both the foil and the glass surface to help prevent the foil from becoming dislodged during the cycle.

Liquid Media
------------

* Fill bottles to no more than two-thirds of their total volume.
* Do not fully tighten bottle caps. Leave them loose enough to vent during sterilization.
* Optionally, cover the cap with aluminum foil.
* Place bottles in the designated autoclave tray.
* Ensure the tray contains approximately 30% water before starting the cycle.
* Refer to the chart in the autoclave room to select the appropriate preset cycle for the bottle size and media volume.

Autoclave Waste
---------------

* Tie the autoclave bag, but not too tightly.
* Puncture several small holes near the top of the bag to allow steam penetration and gas release. Failure to vent the bag may cause the bag to rupture during the cycle.

After the Cycle
---------------

* Allow the pressure to return to normal before opening the door.
* Open the door slowly and stand back to avoid exposure to hot steam.
* Allow liquids to cool before moving them and use heat-resistant gloves when handling hot items.
* Verify that the autoclave tape has changed color before removing the load.
