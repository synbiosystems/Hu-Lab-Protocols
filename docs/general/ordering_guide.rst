Ordering Guide
==============

.. note::
    This ordering guide is based on the current method provided by the business office.

1. Generating Quotes
-----------------

.. Responsibility::
    For Amazon orders and websites that allow web-quote generation, creating the quote falls under each individual lab member's responsibility. Once contacting sales reps is necessary, the burden falls to the lab member in charge of ordering. In that case, send that lab member all necessary information in order to request a quote.

Fisher Scientific: Log in with lab credentials. Create a shopping cart and generate a web quote. This typically produces a contract-priced, tax-exempt quote.

VWR: Contact sales rep through lab email to request a quote.

Sigma-Aldrich and NEB: Log in with lab credentials. Generate quotes through their websites. If tax is applied to the quote, contact sales and request a tax-exempt quote.

Amazon: In a work document, assemble item names, hyperlinks, ASINs (Amazon Standard Identification Number, available if you scroll down and expand 'Item Details'), prices, quantities, and screenshots of each item. This serves as the quote for later submissions to business office.

Primers, gBlocks, Plasmid Sequencing: See below.

Other vendors: Contact the vendor's sales department to request a tax-exempt order quote.

2. PO Forms
---------
.. Responsibility::
    Filling PO Forms is under the responsibility of the lab member in charge of ordering.

PO (purchase order) forms contain vendor name, item number(s), item name(s), unit price(s), quantity(quantities), shipping method/cost, and account number.

PO form is available on business office sharepoint, or in lab Google Drive in 'Purchasing' folder (which is pre-filled with some lab information).

3. Submitting Order Request
------------------------

Once the quote and PO form are ready, submit them through the business office order request form on the CHEN Business Intranet:

'''https://tamucs.sharepoint.com/sites/CHENBusinessIntranet'''

.. note::
    Access to the CHEN Business Intranet requires approval from the business office. Please contact them beforehand if you do not already have access.

After submission, the business office automatically generates an order ID. Enter that order ID, along with all purchase information into the lab Purchase log.

The purchase is routed to the PI for approval via email. This process is relatively quick, since the email conveniently contains all necessary information for the PI.

Once approved, you will be notified via email. At this point, the purchase proceeds to the business office for actually placing the order. Various approvals are obtained in this process. 

When the order is placed, you will be notified via email with an order number. Enter that order number into the Purchase Log. The order number can be entered into the search bar within AggieBuy to see details regarding the order, including the PO number that the vendor will recognize, should you wish to track the order.

Ordering Primers
-----------------

Log into Sigma-Aldrich with lab credentials.

Follow this path:

Products --> Molecular Biology and Functional Genomics --> Oligos, qPCR probes, and peptides --> Custom pre-designed DNA Oligos & qPCR Probes
